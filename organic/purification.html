Purification & Characterisation

Crystallization, Distillation, Chromatography, Detection of Elements

1. Purification Methods

Basic Idea

Organic compounds ko impurities se alag karne ke liye different methods use hote hain, depending on the nature of compound and impurity.

1.1 Crystallization

Crystallization — Impure solid → Hot solvent → Cool → Pure crystals
Working
  • Impure compound ko hot solvent mein dissolve karo
  • Solution ko slowly cool karo
  • Pure compound crystals form karta hai
  • Impurities solution mein hi rehti hain

Example: Sugar, Benzoic acid, Urea

1.2 Sublimation

Sublimation — Solid → Vapor → Solid (Pure)
Working
  • Solid directly vapor mein convert hota hai (without liquid)
  • Vapors cold surface par deposit hote hain
  • Impurities jo volatile nahi hain wo peeche reh jaati hain

Example: Camphor, Naphthalene, Anthracene, Iodine, Benzoic acid

1.3 Distillation

Distillation — Simple, Fractional, Steam

Types of Distillation

TypeUsed ForExample
SimpleLiquids with large BP differenceChloroform + Aniline
FractionalLiquids with close BP difference (< 25 K)Benzene + Toluene
SteamCompounds insoluble in water, volatileAniline, Nitrobenzene
VacuumLiquids with high BP (decompose)Glycerol, Sugar
AzeotropicConstant BP mixtureEthanol + Water

1.4 Chromatography

Chromatography — Separation Principle (Differential Adsorption)

Types of Chromatography

TypeStationary PhaseMobile Phase
PaperWater (in paper)Organic solvent
Thin Layer (TLC)Silica gel / AluminaOrganic solvent
ColumnAlumina / SilicaOrganic solvent
Gas-Liquid (GLC)Liquid on solidGas
Mains Trap
  • Paper Chromatography: Water = Stationary phase
  • TLC: Silica gel = Stationary phase
  • Column: Alumina = Stationary phase

2.1 Detection of Elements

Lassaigne's Test — Detection of N, S, Halogens

3. Quantitative Analysis

3.1 Estimation of Elements

ElementMethodFormula
Carbon & HydrogenLiebig's Method%C = (12/44) × (mass CO₂ / mass compound) × 100
NitrogenKjeldahl's Method%N = (1.4 × N × V) / mass
NitrogenDumas Method%N = (28/22400) × (V at STP / mass) × 100
HalogensCarius Method%X = (atomic mass / AgX molar mass) × (mass AgX / mass) × 100
SulphurCarius Method%S = (32/233) × (mass BaSO₄ / mass) × 100
PhosphorusCarius Method%P = (62/222) × (mass Mg₂P₂O₇ / mass) × 100
Mains Trap
  • Kjeldahl's: Nitrogen estimation (not for nitro/azo compounds)
  • Dumas: Nitrogen estimation (all compounds)
  • Carius: Halogens, Sulphur, Phosphorus

4. Determination of Molecular Formula

Empirical Formula → Molecular Formula
n = Molecular Mass / Empirical Formula Mass
Steps
  1. % composition se moles nikalo
  2. Simplest ratio = Empirical Formula
  3. Molecular Mass (from various methods) / Empirical Formula Mass = n
  4. Molecular Formula = (Empirical Formula) × n

Solved Problems — Purification & Characterisation

🧮 Problem 1: Crystallization

Q: Which method is used to purify sugar?

  1. Sugar ko impurities se alag karna hai
  2. Crystallization best hai
  3. Hot solvent mein dissolve → Cool → Pure crystals
  4. Answer: Crystallization
🧮 Problem 2: Sublimation

Q: Which of the following can be purified by sublimation: Camphor, Sugar, NaCl?

  1. Sublimation: Solid → Vapor directly
  2. Camphor volatile hai
  3. Sugar aur NaCl volatile nahi
  4. Answer: Camphor
🧮 Problem 3: Distillation Type

Q: Benzene (BP 353 K) and Toluene (BP 384 K) ko separate karne ke liye kaunsa method?

  1. BP difference = 384 − 353 = 31 K
  2. 31 > 25 K → Simple distillation
  3. Answer: Simple Distillation
🧮 Problem 4: Steam Distillation

Q: Which compound is purified by steam distillation?

  1. Steam distillation: Insoluble in water, volatile
  2. Aniline steam volatile hai
  3. Answer: Aniline
🧮 Problem 5: Chromatography

Q: In paper chromatography, what is the stationary phase?

  1. Paper chromatography
  2. Stationary phase: Water (in paper)
  3. Mobile phase: Organic solvent
  4. Answer: Water
🧮 Problem 6: Lassaigne's Test

Q: Which colour confirms nitrogen in Lassaigne's test?

  1. NaCN + FeSO₄ + NaOH → Fe₄[Fe(CN)₆]₃
  2. Prussian blue colour
  3. Answer: Prussian Blue
🧮 Problem 7: Functional Group Test

Q: Which test distinguishes phenol from alcohol?

  1. Phenol → FeCl₃ → Violet colour
  2. Alcohol → No colour with FeCl₃
  3. Answer: FeCl₃ Test
🧮 Problem 8: Kjeldahl's Method

Q: Which compound cannot be estimated by Kjeldahl's method?

  1. Kjeldahl's: Nitrogen estimation
  2. Nitro compounds mein nitrogen ring mein hota hai
  3. Wo decompose nahi hota
  4. Answer: Nitrobenzene (Nitro compounds)
🧮 Problem 9: Carius Method

Q: Carius method is used for estimation of:

  1. Halogens, Sulphur, Phosphorus
  2. AgX, BaSO₄, Mg₂P₂O₇ formation
  3. Answer: Halogens, Sulphur, Phosphorus
🧮 Problem 10: Molecular Formula

Q: Empirical formula CH₂O, Molecular mass 180. Find molecular formula.

  1. Empirical formula mass = 12 + 2 + 16 = 30
  2. n = 180 / 30 = 6
  3. Molecular formula = (CH₂O) × 6 = C₆H₁₂O₆
  4. Answer: C₆H₁₂O₆ (Glucose)
