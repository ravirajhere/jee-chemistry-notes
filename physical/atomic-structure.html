Atomic Structure

Bohr Model, Hydrogen Spectrum, de Broglie, Quantum Numbers, Photoelectric Effect

Isotopes, Isobars, Isotones
  • Isotopes: Same Z, different A (e.g., ¹H, ²H, ³H)
  • Isobars: Same A, different Z (e.g., ⁴⁰Ar, ⁴⁰Ca)
  • Isotones: Same number of neutrons

2. Bohr Model of Hydrogen Atom

Postulates
  • Electron fixed orbits mein revolve karta hai (stationary states)
  • Angular momentum quantized: mvr = nh/2π
  • Energy absorbed/released jab electron orbit change karta hai

2.1 Radius of nth Orbit

r_n = 0.529 × n² / Z Å

For H (Z=1): r₁ = 0.529 Å, r₂ = 2.116 Å, r₃ = 4.761 Å

2.2 Energy of nth Orbit

E_n = −13.6 × Z² / n² eV

For H (Z=1): E₁ = −13.6 eV, E₂ = −3.4 eV, E₃ = −1.51 eV

2.3 Velocity of Electron

v_n = 2.18 × 10⁶ × Z / n m/s

For H (Z=1, n=1): v = 2.18 × 10⁶ m/s

2.4 Frequency of Revolution

f = v / 2πr
f ∝ Z² / n³
Mains Trap

Energy negative hoti hai (attraction ke karan)

Radius ∝ n², Energy ∝ −1/n², Velocity ∝ 1/n

3. Hydrogen Spectrum

Rydberg Equation

1/λ = R × (1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)

Jahan R = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹ (Rydberg constant)

n₁ = lower level, n₂ = higher level

3.1 Spectral Series

Seriesn₁Region

Mains Trap

Balmer series → Visible region (n₁ = 2)

Lyman series → UV region (n₁ = 1)

H-alpha line → n = 3 → n = 2 (Balmer)

3.2 Energy Level Diagram (H atom)

  • n = 1 → E = −13.6 eV (Ground state)
  • n = 2 → E = −3.4 eV (First excited state)
  • n = 3 → E = −1.51 eV (Second excited state)
  • n = 4 → E = −0.85 eV
  • n = ∞ → E = 0 (Ionization)

4. de Broglie Wavelength

λ = h / mv

Jahan h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s

4.1 For Accelerated Electron

λ = h / √(2mqV)

Jahan V = accelerating potential, q = charge

λ = 12.27 / √V Å
Mains Trap (2026 PYQ)

Same potential difference (V) par accelerate:

Lighter particle ki wavelength zyada hoti hai

λ ∝ 1/√m (same V par)

5. Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle

Δx × Δp ≥ h / 4π

Position aur momentum ek saath exactly nahi nikal sakte.

7. Electronic Configuration

Rules
  • Aufbau Principle: Lower (n+l) pehle fill
  • Pauli's Exclusion: No two electrons same 4 quantum numbers
  • Hund's Rule: Degenerate orbitals mein pehle single fill
Mains Trap
  • Cr (24): [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹
  • Cu (29): [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹

8. Photoelectric Effect

hν = φ + KE_max

Jahan φ = work function, KE_max = maximum kinetic energy

KE_max = hν − hν₀

Jahan ν₀ = threshold frequency

Mains Trap
  • Work function metal ki property hai
  • ν < ν₀ → No emission (chahe intensity kitni bhi ho)
  • Intensity ↑ → More electrons, but KE same
  • Frequency ↑ → KE ↑

Solved Numericals — Atomic Structure

🧮 Type 1: Radius of nth Orbit

Q: Find radius of 2nd orbit of H atom.

  1. r_n = 0.529 × n² / Z Å
  2. r₂ = 0.529 × 2² / 1
  3. r₂ = 0.529 × 4
  4. r₂ = 2.116 Å
🧮 Type 2: Energy of nth Orbit

Q: Find energy of 2nd orbit of Li²⁺ (Z = 3).

  1. E_n = −13.6 × Z² / n² eV
  2. E₂ = −13.6 × 9 / 4
  3. E₂ = −30.6 eV
🧮 Type 3: Rydberg Equation (Wavelength)

Q: Find wavelength for transition n = 3 → n = 2 in H atom. (R = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹)

  1. 1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)
  2. 1/λ = 1.097 × 10⁷ × (1/4 − 1/9)
  3. 1/λ = 1.097 × 10⁷ × (5/36)
  4. 1/λ = 1.524 × 10⁶ m⁻¹
  5. λ = 656 nm (H-alpha line, Balmer series)
🧮 Type 4: Energy Difference (Transition)

Q: Find energy released in n = 3 → n = 1 transition in H atom.

  1. ΔE = E₃ − E₁ = (−1.51) − (−13.6)
  2. ΔE = 12.09 eV
  3. ΔE = 12.09 eV (or 1.93 × 10⁻¹⁸ J)
🧮 Type 5: de Broglie Wavelength

Q: Find de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated by 100 V.

  1. λ = 12.27 / √V Å
  2. λ = 12.27 / √100
  3. λ = 12.27 / 10
  4. λ = 1.227 Å
🧮 Type 6: de Broglie (Ratio — 2026 PYQ)

Q: Two particles (m and 4m) are accelerated by same V. Find ratio of wavelengths.

  1. λ = h/√(2mqV)
  2. λ ∝ 1/√m (same V par)
  3. λ₁/λ₂ = √(m₂/m₁) = √(4m/m) = 2
  4. λ₁ : λ₂ = 2 : 1
🧮 Type 7: Photoelectric Effect

Q: Work function of metal = 2 eV. Find KE_max for light of 300 nm. (h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴, c = 3 × 10⁸)

  1. E = hc/λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ × 3 × 10⁸) / (300 × 10⁻⁹)
  2. E = 6.626 × 10⁻¹⁹ J = 4.14 eV
  3. KE_max = E − φ = 4.14 − 2
  4. KE_max = 2.14 eV
🧮 Type 8: Quantum Numbers

Q: Find max electrons in n = 3.

  1. Max electrons = 2n²
  2. Max = 2 × 3² = 2 × 9
  3. Max = 18 electrons
🧮 Type 9: Nodes

Q: Find total nodes in 3p orbital.

  1. n = 3, l = 1
  2. Total nodes = n − 1 = 3 − 1
  3. Total nodes = 2
  4. Radial nodes = n − l − 1 = 3 − 1 − 1 = 1
  5. Angular nodes = l = 1
🧮 Type 10: Energy Level Transition

Q: Find ionization energy of H atom from n = 1.

  1. Ionization → n = 1 to n = ∞
  2. E_∞ = 0, E₁ = −13.6 eV
  3. IE = E_∞ − E₁ = 0 − (−13.6)
  4. IE = 13.6 eV
