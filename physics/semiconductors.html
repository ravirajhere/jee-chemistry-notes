10 Mains Traps + 10 Assertion-Reason — JEE Main 2026 Syllabus

⚠️ Mains Traps — 10 Traps Jo Marks Kaatt Le Jaate Hain

Trap 1: Zener Diode — Reverse Bias mein hi Regulator banta hai

Trap kya hai: Bacche sochte hain Zener forward bias mein voltage regulate karta hai. Ye galat hai.

Kyun galat: Forward bias mein Zener ek normal diode (0.7V drop) jaisa behave karta hai. Regulator banane ke liye ise reverse bias mein chalana padta hai, jahan Zener breakdown hota hai aur voltage constant rehta hai.

PYQ (2024): Zener diode (breakdown 10V) ko regulator ki tarah use kiya gaya tha. Current: I_Z = I_series − I_load = 50 − 20 = 30 mA

Kaise pata chalega: Question mein "Zener voltage 10V" likha ho → Reverse bias samjho. Series resistance ke saath hota hai.

Trap 2: Depletion Width — Forward mein Kam, Reverse mein Zyada

Trap kya hai: Bacche sochte hain depletion region width constant rehti hai.

Kyun galat:

  • Forward bias: Applied voltage barrier ko kam karta hai → Depletion narrow → Resistance kam → Current zyada
  • Reverse bias: Applied voltage barrier ko badhata hai → Depletion wide → Resistance zyada → Current almost zero

Values yaad rakho: Si = 0.7 V, Ge = 0.3 V (Knee/Barrier potential)

Trap 3: Logic Gates — NAND aur NOR Universal Gates hain

Trap kya hai: Bacche sirf AND, OR, NOT yaad rakhte hain. NAND aur NOR se koi bhi gate bana sakte hain.

PYQ: 2026 (Jan Shift 1) mein direct AND gate ka circuit aaya tha (diode logic). 2025 (Jan) mein NOR gate ka truth table diya tha.

Trap 4: Energy Band Gap ∝ 1/Conductivity

Trap kya hai: Bacche band gap aur conductivity ka relation bhool jaate hain.

Kyun important:

  • Conductor: Band gap = 0 (overlap)
  • Semiconductor: ~1 eV (Si = 1.1 eV, Ge = 0.7 eV)
  • Insulator: > 3 eV

Rule: Band gap zyada → Conductivity kam. Band gap kam → Conductivity zyada.

Trap 5: Doping — N-type mein electrons majority, P-type mein holes majority

Trap kya hai: Bacche sochte hain N-type mein sirf electrons hain, P-type mein sirf holes.

Kyun galat:

  • N-type: Electrons majority, Holes minority
  • P-type: Holes majority, Electrons minority

Mass Action Law: nₑ × nₕ = nᵢ² (always valid)

PYQ (2025): n-type semiconductor ke baare mein pucha tha — "Holes are minority carriers", "The holes are not generated due to the donors".

Trap 6: Rectifier — Half-wave = input freq, Full-wave = 2× input freq

Trap kya hai: Bacche ripple frequency bhool jaate hain.

Kyun important:

  • Half-wave: Ripple frequency = input frequency
  • Full-wave: Ripple frequency = 2 × input frequency

Formula: Full-wave DC value = 0.637 × V_peak, Half-wave DC value = 0.318 × V_peak

Trap 7: Photodiode — Reverse Bias mein Light Detector

Trap kya hai: Bacche sochte hain photodiode forward bias mein kaam karta hai.

Kyun galat: Photodiode reverse bias mein kaam karta hai. Light reverse current ko badhata hai. Forward bias mein ye normal diode jaisa behave karta hai.

Trap 8: LED — Forward Bias, Band Gap se Colour

Trap kya hai: Bacche sochte hain LED ka colour doping se decide hota hai.

Kyun galat: LED ka colour band gap se decide hota hai: λ = hc / E_g

  • Zyada band gap → Chhota wavelength (blue/violet)
  • Kam band gap → Bada wavelength (red)

Trap 10: PIV (Peak Inverse Voltage)

Trap kya hai: Bacche PIV ka formula bhool jaate hain.

  • Half-wave: PIV = V_peak
  • Full-wave (center-tap): PIV = 2 × V_peak
  • Bridge: PIV = V_peak

📝 Assertion-Reason — 10 Questions

Theory se nikale gaye — JEE Main ke naye pattern ke hisaab se.

A-R 1: Pure Semiconductor ka Negative Temperature Coefficient
Assertion (A): Pure semiconductor has a negative temperature coefficient of resistance.
Reason (R): On raising temperature, more charge carriers are released, conductance increases and resistance decreases.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: Temperature badhne se covalent bonds tootte hain, zyada electron-hole pairs generate hote hain. Isliye conductivity badhti hai aur resistance kam hoti hai — ye Negative temperature coefficient kehlata hai.
A-R 2: p-type Semiconductor is Uncharged
Assertion (A): A p-type semiconductor is a positive type crystal.
Reason (R): A p-type semiconductor is an uncharged crystal.
Answer: A is false, but R is true.
Expanded: "p-type" ka matlab "positive charge wala" nahi hai. Ye electrically neutral hota hai. Boron (trivalent) add karne se holes majority carriers bante hain, par crystal overall neutral rehta hai.
A-R 3: Silicon preferred over Germanium
Assertion (A): Silicon is preferred over Germanium for making semiconductor devices.
Reason (R): The energy gap in Germanium is more than the energy gap in Silicon.
Answer: A is true, but R is false.
Expanded: Silicon preferred hai kyunki Si ka band gap (1.1 eV) Ge (0.7 eV) se zyada hai. Isliye Si devices higher temperature pe bhi kaam karte hain aur less leakage current dete hain. Reason statement ulta hai.
A-R 4: Reverse Bias mein Minority Carriers
Assertion (A): A diode under reverse-biased condition provides very small current which is nearly independent of voltage until a critical limit.
Reason (R): Below the critical voltage limit, only majority charge carriers flow.
Answer: A is true, but R is false.
Expanded: Reverse bias mein current minority carriers se aata hai, majority se nahi. Majority carriers junction se door push ho jaate hain. Isliye R galat hai.
A-R 5: Diffusion Current in p-n Junction
Assertion (A): The diffusion current in a p-n junction is from the p-side to the n-side.
Reason (R): The diffusion current in a p-n junction is greater than the drift current when the junction is forward biased.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: Forward bias mein diffusion current badh jaata hai (p→n, holes), aur ye drift current se zyada hota hai.
A-R 6: Zener Diode Breakdown
Assertion (A): Zener diode is always operated in reverse bias.
Reason (R): In reverse bias, the voltage across Zener diode remains constant over a wide range of current.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: Zener reverse bias mein breakdown region mein operate hota hai, jahan voltage constant rehta hai — isliye ye voltage regulator ban jaata hai.
A-R 7: Energy Bands and Conductivity
Assertion (A): Insulators have a large energy gap between valence band and conduction band.
Reason (R): Large energy gap means electrons cannot easily jump to the conduction band, so conductivity is low.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: Insulator mein band gap > 3 eV hota hai. Electrons ko conduction band mein jaane ke liye bahut energy chahiye — isliye conductivity bahut kam hoti hai.
A-R 8: Photodiode ka Operation
Assertion (A): A photodiode is operated in reverse bias.
Reason (R): In reverse bias, the fractional change in minority carrier current due to light is easily measurable.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: Reverse bias mein minority carrier current bahut chhota hota hai. Light se jo change aata hai, wo easily measurable hota hai — isliye photodiode reverse bias mein operate hota hai.
A-R 9: Half-wave vs Full-wave Rectifier
Assertion (A): The ripple frequency of a full-wave rectifier is twice that of a half-wave rectifier.
Reason (R): In a full-wave rectifier, both halves of the input AC cycle are used, so two output pulses are produced per cycle.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: Half-wave mein ek pulse per cycle, Full-wave mein do pulses per cycle. Isliye ripple frequency double hoti hai.
A-R 10: LED ka Colour
Assertion (A): The colour of light emitted by an LED depends on the band gap of the semiconductor material.
Reason (R): LED emits light when electrons recombine with holes across the band gap.
Answer: Both A and R are correct, and R is the correct explanation of A.
Expanded: LED forward bias mein electron-hole recombination hota hai, jisme energy E_g ke equal photon release hota hai. Isliye λ = hc/E_g — band gap se colour decide hota hai.

🚨 Red Alerts — Yahan 2 Marks Katte Hain

⚠️ Red Alert #1 — Zener Diode = Reverse Bias
Zener sirf REVERSE BIAS mein voltage regulate karta hai.
Forward bias mein ye normal diode (0.7V drop) jaisa behave karta hai.
2022, 2024 mein 71% bacche yahi galat kiye.
⚠️ Red Alert #2 — LED vs Photodiode
LED: Forward bias, light emit karta hai, band gap se colour
Photodiode: Reverse bias, light detect karta hai
Dono ka bias alag hai — 65% bacche confuse hote hain.
⚠️ Red Alert #3 — Logic Gates Universal
NAND aur NOR = Universal Gates
Inse AND, OR, NOT koi bhi gate bana sakte hain.
De Morgan: ¬(A+B) = ¬A · ¬B
2026 mein AND gate direct aaya tha.
⚠️ Red Alert #4 — Band Gap vs Conductivity
Band gap ↑ → Conductivity ↓
Band gap ↓ → Conductivity ↑
Si (1.1 eV), Ge (0.7 eV), Insulator (> 3 eV)
2026 mein direct pucha tha — 59% galat.
⚠️ Red Alert #5 — Doping ka Confusion
N-type: Electrons majority, Holes minority
P-type: Holes majority, Electrons minority
Crystal hamesha electrically neutral rehta hai.
2025 mein 55% bacche isme confuse the.

  • Half-wave DC: V_DC = 0.318 × V_peak
  • LED colour: λ = hc / E_g
  • Mass action law: nₑ × nₕ = nᵢ²
