Electrochemistry

Nernst Equation, Conductance, Faraday's Law, Galvanic Cells, Electrolysis

1. Electrochemical Cells

Galvanic Cell

Chemical energy ko electrical energy mein convert karta hai. Spontaneous reaction.

Example: Daniell Cell (Zn | Zn²⁺ || Cu²⁺ | Cu)

Electrolytic Cell

Electrical energy ko chemical energy mein convert karta hai. Non-spontaneous reaction.

Example: Electrolysis of water, NaCl

1.1 Cell Notation (IUPAC)

  • Anode (left) → Oxidation
  • Cathode (right) → Reduction
  • | → Phase boundary
  • || → Salt bridge
Mains Trap

Anode: Oxidation (Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻)

Cathode: Reduction (Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu)

Galvanic cell: Anode negative, Cathode positive

Electrolytic cell: Anode positive, Cathode negative

2. Electrode Potential & EMF

E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode
E°cell = E°right − E°left
Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE)

E° = 0.00 V at 298 K, 1 atm, 1 M H⁺

Pt | H₂(g, 1 atm) | H⁺(1 M) || ...

Mains Trap

E°cell > 0 → Spontaneous (Galvanic)

E°cell < 0 → Non-spontaneous (Electrolytic)

ΔG° = −nFE°cell

3. Nernst Equation

Ecell = E°cell − (RT/nF) ln Q
Ecell = E°cell − (0.059/n) log Q (at 298 K)

Jahan Q = reaction quotient = [Products] / [Reactants]

Mains Trap
  • 0.059 V = 2.303 RT/F at 298 K
  • For concentration cell: E°cell = 0
  • For equilibrium: Ecell = 0, Q = K

3.1 Nernst Equation at Equilibrium

E°cell = (0.059/n) log K
ΔG° = −nFE°cell = −RT ln K

3.2 Concentration Cell

Ecell = (0.059/n) log(C₁ / C₂)

Jahan C₁ = higher concentration, C₂ = lower concentration

4. Conductance

Definitions
  • Resistance (R): R = ρl/A
  • Conductance (G): G = 1/R
  • Conductivity (κ): κ = 1/ρ = G × (l/A)
  • Molar Conductivity (Λm): Λm = κ × 1000 / M
Λm = κ × 1000 / M

Jahan M = molarity (mol/L), κ = conductivity (S/cm), Λm = S·cm²/mol

4.1 Kohlrausch's Law

Λ°m = ν₊λ°₊ + ν₋λ°₋

Jahan λ°₊, λ°₋ = limiting ionic conductivities, ν₊, ν₋ = number of ions

4.2 Variation with Concentration

  • Strong electrolyte: Λm decreases slightly with concentration
  • Weak electrolyte: Λm decreases sharply with concentration
  • Degree of dissociation (α): α = Λm / Λ°m
Mains Trap

Λm = κ × 1000 / M — Molarity (M) ko concentration ke saath confuse mat karo.

Λ°m = limiting molar conductivity (at infinite dilution)

5. Faraday's Laws of Electrolysis

5.1 First Law

w = Z × I × t

Jahan w = mass deposited, Z = electrochemical equivalent, I = current (A), t = time (s)

5.2 Second Law

w₁/w₂ = E₁/E₂

Jahan E = equivalent weight = Molar mass / n-factor

w = (E × I × t) / 96500
Mains Trap

1 Faraday (F) = 96500 C/mol

Charge = I × t (Coulombs)

Moles of electrons = Charge / 96500

6. Electrolysis & Products

ElectrolyteAnode ProductCathode Product
NaCl (molten)Cl₂Na
NaCl (aq)Cl₂H₂
CuSO₄ (aq)O₂Cu
H₂O (acidified)O₂H₂
Mains Trap (2026 PYQ)

CuSO₄ electrolysis mein O₂ volume nikalna — Faraday's Law se moles of O₂ nikalo, phir volume.

1 mol O₂ = 4 mol electrons (4e⁻ per O₂)

7. Corrosion & Galvanization

Corrosion

Electrochemical process jisme metal oxide ban jaata hai.

Rusting of Iron: Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻ (anode), O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻ (cathode)

Galvanization

Iron ko Zn ki layer se coat karna (sacrificial protection).

Zn pehle oxidize hota hai, Fe ko protect karta hai.

8. Ksp & E° Relationship

E° = (0.059/n) log Ksp

For sparingly soluble salt, E° se Ksp nikal sakte hain, aur vice versa.

Mains Trap (2026 PYQ)

ECl⁻/AgCl/Ag° nikalna tha using Ksp of AgCl and E°Ag⁺/Ag.

Half-cell: AgCl(s) + e⁻ → Ag(s) + Cl⁻(aq)

Solved Numericals — Electrochemistry

🧮 Type 1: E°cell Calculation

Q: Given E°Zn²⁺/Zn = −0.76 V, E°Cu²⁺/Cu = +0.34 V. Find E°cell for Daniell cell.

  1. Anode (oxidation): Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻, E° = −0.76 V
  2. Cathode (reduction): Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu, E° = +0.34 V
  3. E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode
  4. E°cell = 0.34 − (−0.76)
  5. E°cell = +1.10 V
🧮 Type 2: Nernst Equation

Q: For Zn | Zn²⁺(0.1 M) || Cu²⁺(0.01 M) | Cu, E°cell = 1.10 V. Find Ecell.

  1. Reaction: Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu, n = 2
  2. Q = [Zn²⁺] / [Cu²⁺] = 0.1 / 0.01 = 10
  3. Ecell = E°cell − (0.059/n) log Q
  4. Ecell = 1.10 − (0.059/2) log(10)
  5. Ecell = 1.10 − 0.0295 × 1
  6. Ecell = 1.0705 V
🧮 Type 3: Concentration Cell

Q: Find Ecell for Cu | Cu²⁺(1 M) || Cu²⁺(0.01 M) | Cu

  1. Concentration cell → E°cell = 0
  2. Ecell = (0.059/n) log(C₁/C₂)
  3. Ecell = (0.059/2) log(1/0.01)
  4. Ecell = 0.0295 × log(100) = 0.0295 × 2
  5. Ecell = 0.059 V
🧮 Type 4: ΔG° from E°cell

Q: E°cell = 1.10 V, n = 2. Find ΔG°.

  1. ΔG° = −nFE°cell
  2. ΔG° = −2 × 96500 × 1.10
  3. ΔG° = −212300 J = −212.3 kJ
  4. Negative ΔG° → Spontaneous
🧮 Type 5: Molar Conductivity

Q: Conductivity of 0.1 M KCl is 0.0129 S/cm. Find Λm.

  1. Λm = κ × 1000 / M
  2. Λm = 0.0129 × 1000 / 0.1
  3. Λm = 129 S·cm²/mol
🧮 Type 6: Degree of Dissociation

Q: Λm = 100 S·cm²/mol, Λ°m = 200 S·cm²/mol. Find α.

  1. α = Λm / Λ°m
  2. α = 100 / 200
  3. α = 0.5 = 50%
🧮 Type 7: Faraday's Law (Mass Deposited)

Q: Find mass of Cu deposited when 2 A current flows for 30 min through CuSO₄. (M(Cu) = 63.5, n = 2)

  1. t = 30 × 60 = 1800 s
  2. Charge = I × t = 2 × 1800 = 3600 C
  3. Moles e⁻ = 3600 / 96500 = 0.0373 mol
  4. Moles Cu = 0.0373 / 2 = 0.01865 mol
  5. Mass Cu = 0.01865 × 63.5
  6. Mass = 1.184 g
🧮 Type 8: Faraday's Law (Volume of Gas)

Q (2026 PYQ): Find volume of O₂ at STP when 0.1 A flows for 9650 s through CuSO₄.

  1. Charge = I × t = 0.1 × 9650 = 965 C
  2. Moles e⁻ = 965 / 96500 = 0.01 mol
  3. For O₂: 4e⁻ → 1 O₂ → 0.01/4 = 0.0025 mol O₂
  4. Volume = 0.0025 × 22.4 L
  5. Volume = 0.056 L = 56 mL
🧮 Type 9: E°cell and K

Q: E°cell = 0.59 V, n = 1. Find K.

  1. E°cell = (0.059/n) log K
  2. 0.59 = (0.059/1) log K
  3. log K = 10
  4. K = 10¹⁰
🧮 Type 10: Ksp from E°

Q (2026 PYQ): Find Ksp of AgCl if E°AgCl/Ag = 0.22 V and E°Ag⁺/Ag = 0.80 V (at 298 K).

  1. AgCl(s) + e⁻ → Ag(s) + Cl⁻(aq), E° = 0.22 V
  2. Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag(s), E° = 0.80 V
  3. Subtract: AgCl → Ag⁺ + Cl⁻, E° = 0.22 − 0.80 = −0.58 V
  4. E° = (0.059/n) log Ksp
  5. −0.58 = 0.059 × log Ksp
  6. log Ksp = −9.83
  7. Ksp = 1.5 × 10⁻¹⁰
