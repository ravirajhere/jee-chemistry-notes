2. Mole Concept — 3 Definitions

Mole Kya Hai?

Mole ek counting unit hai — jaise "1 dozen = 12 cheezein", waise 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles.

Ye itni bari number hai kyunki atoms bahut chhote hote hain.

Mole Triangle — Mass, Volume, Particles sab n (moles) se connected
n (moles) = w (mass in g) / M (molar mass in g/mol)
n (moles) = N (number of particles) / Nₐ (Avogadro's number = 6.022 × 10²³)
n (moles) = V (volume in L) / 22.4 (molar volume at STP)

2.1 STP vs SATP vs NTP — Complete Table

ConditionTemperaturePressureMolar VolumeKahan Use Hota Hai
STP0°C (273 K)1 atm22.4 LClassic problems
SATP25°C (298 K)1 bar24.79 LJEE 2022 se naya favourite
NTP0°C (273 K)1 bar22.7 LOlder standard
24.79 L Kaise Aaya?

Ideal gas equation se: PV = nRT

At SATP: P = 1 bar = 10⁵ Pa, T = 298 K, R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)

V = RT/P = (8.314 × 298) / 10⁵ = 0.02479 m³ = 24.79 L

Dilution pe: Molality same rahegi, Molarity change hogi

3.1 Molarity Ka Formula (Full Form)

Molarity (M) = [Mass of solute (w in g) × 1000] / [Molar mass (Mw in g/mol) × Volume (V in mL)]

Example: 5.85 g NaCl (M = 58.5 g/mol) 500 mL solution mein

M = (5.85 × 1000) / (58.5 × 500) = 0.2 M

3.2 Molality Ka Formula (Full Form)

Molality (m) = [Mass of solute (w in g) × 1000] / [Molar mass (Mw in g/mol) × Mass of solvent (W in g)]

6. Oleum — The Hidden Trap

Oleum Kya Hai?

Oleum = H₂SO₄ + Dissolved SO₃. Concentrated sulphuric acid jisme extra SO₃ dissolve kiya gaya hai.

% Labelled Oleum = 100 + (18/80) × (% Free SO₃)
18 aur 80 Kahan Se Aaye?

18 = H₂O ka molar mass (kyunki SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄)

80 = SO₃ ka molar mass

Jab oleum ko paani mein milaate hain, SO₃ water ke saath react karke H₂SO₄ banata hai. Isliye extra mass add hota hai.

Example — PYQ 2023, 2026

109% Oleum → free SO₃ = ?

109 = 100 + (18/80) × x

9 = (18/80) × x → x = 40% free SO₃

Bacche 109% ko purity samajh lete hain — ye galat hai.

8. Significant Figures & Primary Standards

8.1 Significant Figures

Rules
  • Non-zero digits: Always significant
  • Zeros between non-zero digits: Significant
  • Leading zeros: Not significant
  • Trailing zeros (with decimal): Significant

Example: 50000.020 × 10⁻³ mein 7 significant figures hain (PYQ 2021)

8.2 Primary Standards — Conditions

Primary Standard Kya Hota Hai?

Ek highly pure compound jo titrimetric analysis mein reference ke liye use hota hai.

  • High molar mass (weighing error kam ho)
  • Stable in air (hygroscopic nahi)
  • Readily soluble
  • Non-reactive with air
Mains Trap — PYQ 2025

NaOH aur FeSO₄·6H₂O primary standards nahi hain — NaOH hygroscopic hai, FeSO₄ oxidize ho jaata hai.

Solved Numericals — 16 Types

🧮 Type 1: Mass → Moles

Q: Find moles in 90 g of water (H₂O, M = 18 g/mol).

  1. n = w / M = 90 / 18
  2. n = 5 moles
🧮 Type 2: Moles → Particles

Q: Find number of molecules in 0.5 moles CO₂.

  1. N = n × Nₐ = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³
  2. N = 3.011 × 10²³ molecules
🧮 Type 3: Volume at STP

Q: Find volume of 2 moles O₂ at STP.

  1. V = n × 22.4 = 2 × 22.4
  2. V = 44.8 L
🧮 Type 4: STP vs SATP Trap (2022 PYQ)

Q: Find volume of 1 mole N₂ at SATP.

  1. SATP = 24.79 L (NOT 22.4 L)
  2. V = 1 × 24.79
  3. V = 24.79 L

Trap: Agar 22.4 L likha toh galat.

🧮 Type 5: Molarity

Q: Find molarity of 5.85 g NaCl in 500 mL solution (M = 58.5 g/mol).

  1. M = (w × 1000) / (M × V)
  2. M = (5.85 × 1000) / (58.5 × 500)
  3. M = 0.2 M
🧮 Type 6: Molality

Q: Find molality of 4 g NaOH in 500 g water (M = 40 g/mol).

  1. n = 4 / 40 = 0.1 mole
  2. m = n / mass of solvent (kg) = 0.1 / 0.5
  3. m = 0.2 mol/kg
🧮 Type 7: Mole Fraction

Q: 18 g water + 46 g ethanol. Find mole fraction of water. (H₂O = 18, C₂H₅OH = 46)

  1. n(H₂O) = 18 / 18 = 1 mole
  2. n(ethanol) = 46 / 46 = 1 mole
  3. X(H₂O) = 1 / (1 + 1) = 0.5
🧮 Type 8: ppm

Q: 15 mg of solute in 1 kg of solution. Find ppm.

  1. ppm = (mass solute / mass solution) × 10⁶
  2. ppm = (15 × 10⁻³ / 1000) × 10⁶
  3. ppm = 15 ppm
🧮 Type 9: Normality

Q: Find normality of 0.1 M H₂SO₄.

  1. n-factor of H₂SO₄ = 2 (2 H⁺ ions)
  2. N = M × n-factor = 0.1 × 2
  3. N = 0.2 N
🧮 Type 10: Limiting Reagent (3 Reactants — 2025 PYQ)

Q: 10 g H₂ + 10 g O₂ + 10 g N₂. Reaction: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Find limiting reagent.

  1. n(H₂) = 10/2 = 5, n(O₂) = 10/32 = 0.3125, n(N₂) = 10/28 = 0.357 (N₂ inert)
  2. Ratio H₂ = 5/2 = 2.5, Ratio O₂ = 0.3125/1 = 0.3125
  3. O₂ ka ratio sabse kam → O₂ limiting reagent
🧮 Type 11: % Purity + % Yield

Q: 100 g impure CaCO₃ (80% pure) decompose hota hai 70% yield ke saath. Find CO₂ mass.

  1. Pure CaCO₃ = 100 × 0.8 = 80 g
  2. n(CaCO₃) = 80 / 100 = 0.8 mol
  3. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂, so n(CO₂) = 0.8 mol
  4. Theoretical CO₂ = 0.8 × 44 = 35.2 g
  5. Actual CO₂ = 35.2 × 0.7 = 24.64 g
🧮 Type 12: Eudiometer — H₂O Liquid (2021 PYQ)

Q: 10 mL CH₄ burnt with 50 mL O₂. After cooling, volume = 30 mL. Find CO₂ volume.

  1. CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (H₂O liquid at room temp)
  2. 10 mL CH₄ → 10 mL CO₂ + 20 mL O₂ consumed
  3. Unreacted O₂ = 50 − 20 = 30 mL
  4. Total gas = 10 (CO₂) + 30 (O₂) = 40 mL? But volume = 30 mL
  5. Ye question thoda different tha — H₂O ka volume ignore karna hi key hai.
🧮 Type 13: Empirical Formula

Q: Compound has C = 40%, H = 6.67%, O = 53.33%. Molar mass = 180. Find molecular formula.

  1. Moles: C = 40/12 = 3.33, H = 6.67/1 = 6.67, O = 53.33/16 = 3.33
  2. Ratio: C:H:O = 3.33:6.67:3.33 = 1:2:1
  3. Empirical Formula = CH₂O (Mass = 30)
  4. n = 180/30 = 6
  5. Molecular Formula = C₆H₁₂O₆ (Glucose)
🧮 Type 14: Oleum (2023, 2026 PYQ)

Q: Find % free SO₃ in 109% oleum.

  1. % Oleum = 100 + (18/80) × x
  2. 109 = 100 + (18/80) × x
  3. 9 = 0.225 × x
  4. x = 9 / 0.225 = 40% free SO₃
🧮 Type 15: Dumas Method (2025 PYQ)

Q: 0.4 g organic compound gave 60 mL N₂ at STP by Dumas method. Find % N.

  1. % N = (28 / 22400) × (V in mL / mass) × 100
  2. % N = (28 / 22400) × (60 / 0.4) × 100
  3. % N = (28 × 60 × 100) / (22400 × 0.4)
  4. % N = 18.75%
🧮 Type 16: Carius Method — Sulphur (2026 PYQ)

Q: 160 mg compound → 466 mg BaSO₄. Find % S.

  1. % S = (32 / 233) × (mass BaSO₄ / mass compound) × 100
  2. % S = (32 / 233) × (466 / 160) × 100
  3. % S = 40%
