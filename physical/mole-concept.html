Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry

Mole Concept, Stoichiometry, Limiting Reagent, Concentration Terms

1. Mole Concept

Definition

1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles (Avogadro's number)

1 mole of any substance ka mass = Molar Mass (g/mol)

1.1 Important Relations

Moles (n) = Given Mass (w) / Molar Mass (M)
Moles (n) = Number of Particles / 6.022 × 10²³
Moles (n) = Volume at STP (L) / 22.4
Mains Trap

1 mole gas at STP = 22.4 L (273 K, 1 atm)

1 mole gas at NTP = 22.7 L (273 K, 1 bar)

2. Atomic Mass & Molecular Mass

TermDefinition
Atomic MassAverage mass of atom (in amu)
Molecular MassSum of atomic masses of all atoms
Formula MassFor ionic compounds (e.g., NaCl)
Equivalent MassMolar Mass / n-factor

2.1 Average Atomic Mass

Average Mass = (M₁ × % + M₂ × %) / 100

3. Percentage Composition

% of element = (Mass of element in compound / Molar Mass) × 100

3.1 Empirical & Molecular Formula

TermDefinition
Empirical FormulaSimplest whole-number ratio of atoms
Molecular FormulaActual number of atoms
Molecular Formula = (Empirical Formula) × n
n = Molar Mass / Empirical Formula Mass

4. Stoichiometry

Definition

Balanced chemical equation se reactant aur product ke quantitative relation ka study.

4.1 Limiting Reagent

Concept

Wo reactant jo pehle khatam ho jaata hai aur reaction ko rok deta hai.

Limiting Reagent nikalne ka tarika:

  • Har reactant ke moles ko uske stoichiometric coefficient se divide karo
  • Jiska ratio sabse kam ho, wo limiting reagent hai

4.2 Percentage Yield

% Yield = (Actual Yield / Theoretical Yield) × 100

5. Concentration Terms

TermFormulaUnit

Mains Trap

Molarity temperature-dependent hai (volume change hota hai)

Molality temperature-independent hai (mass change nahi hota)

5.1 Normality & Equivalent Weight

Equivalent Weight = Molar Mass / n-factor
Normality = Molarity × n-factor

5.2 n-factor for Acids, Bases, Salts

Typen-factor
AcidBasicity (H⁺ ions)
BaseAcidity (OH⁻ ions)
SaltTotal charge on cation/anion
RedoxChange in oxidation number

6. Significant Figures & Laws

Laws of Chemical Combination
  • Law of Conservation of Mass: Mass na create hota hai na destroy
  • Law of Definite Proportions: Compound ka composition fixed
  • Law of Multiple Proportions: Different compounds mein mass ratio whole number
  • Gay-Lussac's Law: Gaseous volumes simple ratio mein
  • Avogadro's Law: Equal volumes mein equal molecules (same T, P)

Solved Numericals — Mole Concept

🧮 Type 1: Moles Calculation

Q: Find moles in 90 g of water.

  1. Molar Mass of H₂O = 18 g/mol
  2. n = w / M = 90 / 18
  3. n = 5 moles
🧮 Type 2: Number of Particles

Q: Find number of molecules in 0.5 moles of CO₂.

  1. N = n × Avogadro's Number
  2. N = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³
  3. N = 3.011 × 10²³ molecules
🧮 Type 3: Volume at STP

Q: Find volume of 2 moles of O₂ at STP.

  1. Volume = n × 22.4 L
  2. Volume = 2 × 22.4
  3. Volume = 44.8 L
🧮 Type 4: Empirical & Molecular Formula

Q: Compound has C = 40%, H = 6.67%, O = 53.33%. Molar mass = 180. Find molecular formula.

  1. Moles: C = 40/12 = 3.33, H = 6.67/1 = 6.67, O = 53.33/16 = 3.33
  2. Ratio: C:H:O = 3.33:6.67:3.33 = 1:2:1
  3. Empirical Formula = CH₂O (Mass = 30)
  4. n = 180 / 30 = 6
  5. Molecular Formula = C₆H₁₂O₆
🧮 Type 5: Limiting Reagent

Q: 10 g H₂ reacts with 10 g O₂ to form H₂O. Find limiting reagent.

  1. 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
  2. Moles H₂ = 10/2 = 5, Moles O₂ = 10/32 = 0.3125
  3. Ratio H₂ = 5/2 = 2.5, Ratio O₂ = 0.3125/1 = 0.3125
  4. O₂ ka ratio kam hai → Limiting Reagent = O₂
🧮 Type 6: Percentage Yield

Q: Theoretical yield = 50 g, Actual yield = 40 g. Find % yield.

  1. % Yield = (Actual / Theoretical) × 100
  2. % Yield = (40 / 50) × 100
  3. % Yield = 80%
🧮 Type 7: Molarity

Q: Find molarity of 5.85 g NaCl in 500 mL solution.

  1. Moles NaCl = 5.85 / 58.5 = 0.1 mol
  2. Volume = 0.5 L
  3. M = 0.1 / 0.5
  4. M = 0.2 M
🧮 Type 8: Molality

Q: Find molality of 4 g NaOH in 500 g water.

  1. Moles NaOH = 4 / 40 = 0.1 mol
  2. Mass solvent = 0.5 kg
  3. m = 0.1 / 0.5
  4. m = 0.2 mol/kg
🧮 Type 9: Normality

Q: Find normality of 0.1 M H₂SO₄.

  1. n-factor of H₂SO₄ = 2 (2 H⁺ ions)
  2. N = M × n-factor
  3. N = 0.1 × 2
  4. N = 0.2 N
🧮 Type 10: Average Atomic Mass

Q: Cl has isotopes ³⁵Cl (75%) and ³⁷Cl (25%). Find average mass.

  1. Average = (35 × 75 + 37 × 25) / 100
  2. Average = (2625 + 925) / 100
  3. Average = 3550 / 100
  4. Average = 35.5 amu
