2. Electronic Effects

2.1 Inductive Effect (I)

Definition

Sigma bond ke through electron displacement, electronegativity difference ke karan.

  • −I Effect: Electron withdrawing (NO₂, CN, Cl, F, COOH)
  • +I Effect: Electron donating (CH₃, C₂H₅, alkyl groups)
−I Effect Order: −NO₂ > −CN > −COOH > −F > −Cl > −Br > −I
+I Effect Order: −C(CH₃)₃ > −CH(CH₃)₂ > −CH₂CH₃ > −CH₃

2.2 Resonance (Mesomeric Effect)

Definition

Pi electrons ka delocalization conjugated system mein.

  • +M Effect: Electron donating (−OH, −NH₂, −OR, −Cl)
  • −M Effect: Electron withdrawing (−NO₂, −CN, −CHO, −COOH)
Benzene — Resonance Hybrid (6π electrons)

2.3 Hyperconjugation

Definition

Sigma C−H bond ke electrons ka delocalization empty p-orbital ya pi-system ke saath.

Requirement: Alpha hydrogen (adjacent carbon par H) hona chahiye

Hyperconjugation — More α-H = More Stable

3. Stability of Intermediates

3.1 Carbocation (R⁺)

Stability OrderReason
3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃⁺Hyperconjugation + Inductive effect
Benzyl > Allyl > 3°Resonance stabilisation

3.2 Carbanion (R⁻)

Stability OrderReason
CH₃⁻ > 1° > 2° > 3°Inductive effect (−I stabilizes)
sp > sp² > sp³s-character zyada = more electronegative = stable
Mains Trap (2026 PYQ)

Carbanion Stability: sp > sp² > sp³

Example: HC≡C⁻ > H₂C=CH⁻ > CH₃CH₂⁻

3.3 Free Radical (R•)

Stability: 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃• (same as carbocation)

3.4 Carbene & Nitrene

Carbene (:CH₂)
  • Neutral species, 6 electrons (2 unpaired + 1 lone pair)
  • Singlet: sp², paired electrons, diamagnetic
  • Triplet: sp, unpaired electrons, paramagnetic
  • Highly reactive intermediate

5. Tautomerism (Keto-Enol)

Keto-Enol Tautomerism (α-H required)
Mains Trap

Enol form zyada stable hota hai agar:

  • Resonance stabilisation ho
  • Intramolecular H-bonding ho

7.2 Stereoisomerism

Optical Isomerism
  • Chiral carbon: 4 different groups attached
  • Enantiomers: Non-superimposable mirror images
  • Diastereomers: Non-mirror image stereoisomers
  • Meso compound: Achiral despite chiral carbons (internal symmetry)

7.3 CIP Rules (R/S Nomenclature)

Steps
  1. Priority assign karo (atomic number ke hisaab se)
  2. Lowest priority group ko peeche rakho
  3. 1 → 2 → 3 ka direction dekho
  4. Clockwise → R, Anti-clockwise → S

7.4 E/Z Nomenclature

Rule
  • E (Entgegen): Higher priority groups opposite sides
  • Z (Zusammen): Higher priority groups same side

9. Bond Length, Bond Angle & Dipole Moment

Bond Angle: sp (180°) > sp² (120°) > sp³ (109.5°)
Bond Length: C−C (1.54 Å) > C=C (1.34 Å) > C≡C (1.20 Å)

10. IUPAC Nomenclature

Basic Rules
  1. Longest chain identify karo
  2. Lowest locant rule follow karo
  3. Alphabetical order mein substituents likho
  4. Prefixes: di, tri, tetra (same substituent ke liye)

Solved Problems — GOC

🧮 Problem 1: Carbanion Stability (2026 PYQ)

Q: Arrange in decreasing order of stability: CH₃⁻, HC≡C⁻, H₂C=CH⁻

  1. Stability ∝ s-character
  2. HC≡C⁻ → sp (50% s-character)
  3. H₂C=CH⁻ → sp² (33% s-character)
  4. CH₃⁻ → sp³ (25% s-character)
  5. Answer: HC≡C⁻ > H₂C=CH⁻ > CH₃⁻
🧮 Problem 2: Acidity Comparison

Q: Which is more acidic: Phenol or Ethanol?

  1. Phenol mein resonance stabilised phenoxide ion
  2. Ethanol mein resonance nahi
  3. Answer: Phenol > Ethanol
🧮 Problem 3: Hyperconjugation

Q: Which is more stable: 1-Butene or 2-Butene?

  1. 2-Butene: 6 alpha H
  2. 1-Butene: 3 alpha H
  3. Answer: 2-Butene > 1-Butene
🧮 Problem 4: Tautomerism

Q: Why is enol form of acetylacetone more stable?

  1. Acetylacetone: CH₃COCH₂COCH₃
  2. Enol form mein conjugation + intramolecular H-bonding
  3. 6-membered chelate ring banta hai
  4. Answer: Conjugation + H-bonding ke karan
🧮 Problem 5: Conformational Isomerism

Q: Which conformation of n-butane is most stable?

  1. Anti conformation (180° dihedral angle)
  2. Gauche (60°) se zyada stable
  3. Answer: Anti conformation
🧮 Problem 6: R/S Nomenclature

Q: Assign R/S to 2-butanol.

  1. Priority: OH > C₂H₅ > CH₃ > H
  2. H peeche rakho
  3. OH → C₂H₅ → CH₃ direction dekho
  4. Clockwise → R, Anti-clockwise → S
🧮 Problem 7: Aromaticity

Q: Is cyclopentadienyl anion aromatic?

  1. Cyclic: Yes
  2. Planar: Yes
  3. Conjugated: Yes
  4. π electrons = 6 (4n+2, n=1)
  5. Answer: Aromatic
🧮 Problem 8: Basicity

Q: Arrange in decreasing order of basicity: NH₃, CH₃NH₂, (CH₃)₂NH, (CH₃)₃N

  1. Aqueous solution: 2° > 1° > 3° > NH₃
  2. Solvation aur steric hindrance ka balance
  3. Answer: (CH₃)₂NH > CH₃NH₂ > (CH₃)₃N > NH₃
🧮 Problem 9: Stability of Alkenes

Q: Which is most stable: 1-butene, cis-2-butene, trans-2-butene?

  1. Heat of hydrogenation lower = More stable
  2. Trans-2-butene sabse stable (less steric hindrance)
  3. Answer: trans-2-butene > cis-2-butene > 1-butene
🧮 Problem 10: E/Z Nomenclature

Q: Assign E/Z to 2-butene isomers.

  1. Cis-2-butene: CH₃ groups same side → Z
  2. Trans-2-butene: CH₃ groups opposite → E
  3. Answer: Cis = Z, Trans = E

📋 Complete Concept Sheet — GOC

Saare concepts aur orders ek jagah — category-wise. Print karke revision ke liye use karo.

1. BOND FISSION
Homolytic: Free radical (UV light, Peroxide)
Heterolytic: Carbocation + Carbanion (Polar solvent)
2. NUCLEOPHILES & ELECTROPHILES
Nucleophile: Electron pair deta hai (OH⁻, CN⁻, NH₃)
Electrophile: Electron pair leta hai (H⁺, NO₂⁺, AlCl₃)
3. ELECTRONIC EFFECTS
Inductive (−I): −NO₂ > −CN > −COOH > −F > −Cl > −Br > −I
Inductive (+I): −C(CH₃)₃ > −CH(CH₃)₂ > −CH₂CH₃ > −CH₃
Resonance (+M): −OH, −NH₂, −OR, −Cl
Resonance (−M): −NO₂, −CN, −CHO, −COOH
Hyperconjugation: More αH = More stable
4. STABILITY OF INTERMEDIATES
Carbocation: 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃⁺
Carbanion: CH₃⁻ > 1° > 2° > 3°
Carbanion (Hybridisation): sp > sp² > sp³
Free Radical: 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃•
5. ACIDITY
General Order: Carboxylic Acid > Phenol > Water > Alcohol
ARIO Rule: Atom, Resonance, Inductive, Orbital
−I effect: Acidity badhata hai
+I effect: Acidity ghatata hai
6. BASICITY
Aliphatic Amines (aqueous): 2° > 1° > 3° > NH₃
Aliphatic Amines (gas): 3° > 2° > 1° > NH₃
+I effect: Basicity badhata hai
−I/−M effect: Basicity ghatata hai
7. ISOMERISM
Structural: Chain, Position, Functional, Metamerism, Tautomerism, Ring Chain
Geometrical: C=C ya ring (restricted rotation)
Optical: Chiral carbon (4 different groups)
Conformational: Staggered > Eclipsed, Anti > Gauche
Meso: Achiral despite chiral carbons
8. AROMATICITY
Huckel's Rule: (4n+2) π electrons
Conditions: Cyclic + Planar + Conjugated
Benzene: 6π, Aromatic
Cyclobutadiene: 4π, Anti-aromatic
Cyclooctatetraene: 8π, Non-planar, Non-aromatic
9. BOND LENGTH & ANGLE
Bond Length: C−C (1.54 Å) > C=C (1.34 Å) > C≡C (1.20 Å)
Bond Angle: sp (180°) > sp² (120°) > sp³ (109.5°)
Dipole Moment: Symmetric → μ = 0
10. IUPAC NOMENCLATURE
Longest chain identify karo
Lowest locant rule follow karo
Alphabetical order mein substituents
Prefixes: di, tri, tetra (same substituent)
