Qualitative Analysis (Salt Analysis)

Cation Groups, Anion Groups, Confirmatory Tests, Flame Tests, PYQ Analysis

1. Basic Principles

Definition

Qualitative salt analysis mein inorganic salt ke cations (basic radicals) aur anions (acidic radicals) ki identify ki jaati hai.

1.1 Important Principles

PrincipleUse
Solubility ProductPrecipitation tab hota hai jab ionic product > Ksp
Common Ion EffectPrecipitation ko control karta hai
Complex FormationConfirmatory tests mein use hota hai
Mains Trap

Solubility product ka concept important hai — jab ionic product Ksp se zyada ho jaata hai, tab precipitate milta hai.

2. Preliminary Tests

2.1 Dry Tests

TestKya Pata Chalta Hai
Colour of SaltBlue → Cu²⁺, Green → Ni²⁺/Fe²⁺, Brown → Fe³⁺
OdourAmmoniacal → NH₄⁺, Vinegar → CH₃COO⁻, Rotten egg → S²⁻
Flame TestBrick red → Ca²⁺, Green → Ba²⁺, Crimson → Sr²⁺
Borax Bead TestCu²⁺ → Blue, Fe³⁺ → Yellow-brown

2.2 Wet Test with Dilute HCl

Gas EvolvedAnion
CO₂ (colourless, lime water milky)CO₃²⁻
SO₂ (suffocating smell)SO₃²⁻
H₂S (rotten egg smell)S²⁻
NO₂ (brown gas)NO₂⁻
CH₃COOH (vinegar smell)CH₃COO⁻
Mains Trap
  • Colour se directly cation pata chal sakta hai — Blue = Cu²⁺, Green = Ni²⁺, Brown = Fe³⁺
  • Flame test mein Ca²⁺ (brick red), Ba²⁺ (apple green), Sr²⁺ (crimson)

3. Cation Analysis (Group-wise)

3.1 Group Reagents Table

GroupGroup ReagentCations
Group IDilute HClPb²⁺, Ag⁺, Hg₂²⁺
Group IIH₂S in acidic mediumCu²⁺, Pb²⁺, Hg²⁺, Bi³⁺
Group IIINH₄Cl + NH₄OHAl³⁺, Fe³⁺, Cr³⁺
Group IVH₂S in basic mediumNi²⁺, Co²⁺, Mn²⁺, Zn²⁺
Group V(NH₄)₂CO₃Ca²⁺, Ba²⁺, Sr²⁺
Group VI—Mg²⁺, NH₄⁺

3.2 Important Group Reagents

  • Group I: Dilute HCl → PbCl₂ (white ppt)
  • Group II: H₂S + HCl → CuS (black), PbS (black), HgS (black)
  • Group III: NH₄OH → Al(OH)₃ (gelatinous white), Fe(OH)₃ (reddish brown)
  • Group IV: H₂S + NH₄OH → NiS (black), ZnS (white)
  • Group V: (NH₄)₂CO₃ → CaCO₃ (white), BaCO₃ (white)
Mains Trap
  • Group reagent yaad rakho — Group I = HCl, Group II = H₂S (acidic), Group III = NH₄OH, Group IV = H₂S (basic), Group V = (NH₄)₂CO₃
  • Pb²⁺ dono Group I aur Group II mein aata hai

4.1 Anion Groups

Group BConcentrated H₂SO₄Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, NO₃⁻, C₂O₄²⁻
Group C—SO₄²⁻, PO₄³⁻

4.2 Confirmatory Tests

AnionTestObservation
CO₃²⁻Dil. HClCO₂ gas (lime water milky)
S²⁻Lead acetate paperBlack ppt (PbS)
SO₄²⁻BaCl₂White ppt (BaSO₄), insoluble in conc. HCl
Cl⁻AgNO₃White ppt (AgCl), soluble in NH₄OH
Br⁻AgNO₃Pale yellow ppt (AgBr), partially soluble
I⁻AgNO₃Yellow ppt (AgI), insoluble in NH₄OH
NO₃⁻Brown ring testBrown ring at junction
CH₃COO⁻FeCl₃Blood red colour
Mains Trap
  • SO₄²⁻ → BaCl₂ se white ppt, jo conc. HCl mein insoluble hota hai
  • Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻ → AgNO₃ se white, pale yellow, yellow ppt respectively. Solubility in NH₄OH ka difference yaad rakho

Mains Traps — Qualitative Analysis

1. Cation Group Reagents

Group I: Dilute HCl

Group II: H₂S (acidic)

Group III: NH₄Cl + NH₄OH

Group IV: H₂S (basic)

Group V: (NH₄)₂CO₃

2. Flame Colours

Ca²⁺ → Brick Red

Ba²⁺ → Apple Green

Sr²⁺ → Crimson

Na⁺ → Golden Yellow

K⁺ → Lilac

3. Fe³⁺ Test

KSCN → Blood Red colour

4. Cu²⁺ Test

K₄[Fe(CN)₆] → Chocolate Brown ppt

5. SO₄²⁻ Test

BaCl₂ → White ppt (BaSO₄), insoluble in conc. HCl

6. AgNO₃ Tests (Halides)

Cl⁻ → White ppt (AgCl), soluble in NH₄OH

Br⁻ → Pale Yellow ppt (AgBr), partially soluble

I⁻ → Yellow ppt (AgI), insoluble in NH₄OH

7. Brown Ring Test

NO₃⁻ → Brown ring at junction

8. NH₄⁺ Test

NaOH → NH₃ gas (pungent smell)

9. Pb²⁺ Special

Pb²⁺ dono Group I aur Group II mein aata hai

10. Blood Red Colour

CH₃COO⁻ + FeCl₃ → Blood red colour
