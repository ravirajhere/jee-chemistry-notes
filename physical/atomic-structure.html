1. Bohr Model of Hydrogen Atom

Bohr Kya Kehta Hai?

Electron nucleus ke around fixed orbits mein revolve karta hai (stationary states). Har orbit ki fixed energy hoti hai.

Bohr Model — Radius badhta hai, Energy kam negative hoti hai

1.1 Bohr Model — 3 Formulas

Radius: rₙ = 0.529 × n² / Z Å
Energy: Eₙ = −13.6 × Z² / n² eV
Velocity: vₙ = 2.18 × 10⁶ × Z / n m/s
Frequency of Revolution: f = 6.6 × 10¹⁵ × Z² / n³ Hz
Mains Trap — 2026 PYQ

Li²⁺ (Z=3), He⁺ (Z=2) ke liye Z lagana zaroori hai. 2026 mein Z bhoolne pe 82% galat.

Frequency of Revolution 2026 mein direct aaya — formula yaad rakho.

1.2 Bohr Model Ki Limitations

  • Multi-electron atoms ke liye kaam nahi karta
  • Zeeman effect (magnetic field) explain nahi karta
  • Stark effect (electric field) explain nahi karta
  • Heisenberg's uncertainty principle violate karta hai

2. Hydrogen Spectrum

Hydrogen Spectrum — Lyman (UV), Balmer (Visible), Paschen (IR)
Rydberg Equation: 1/λ = R × Z² × (1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)

Jahan R = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹ (Rydberg constant)

2.1 Number of Spectral Lines

Formula (Trap Alert)

Ground state se (n₁ = 1): n(n−1)/2

Otherwise (n₂ → n₁): (n₂−n₁)(n₂−n₁+1)/2

Mains Trap — 2026 PYQ

4th se 2nd level pe kitni lines? (4−2)(4−2+1)/2 = 3 lines

Agar n(n−1)/2 lagaya toh 6 aa jayega — galat.

3.1 n + l Rule (Aufbau Principle)

Rule

Pehle n+l kam wala orbital fill hota hai. Agar n+l same ho, toh n kam wala pehle.

4s (n+l=4) < 3d (n+l=5) < 4p (n+l=5)

4. de Broglie Wavelength

λ = h / mv
λ = h / √(2mKE) = h / √(2mqV)
λ = 12.27 / √V Å (accelerated electron)
Mains Trap — 2024 PYQ

Same potential difference (V) par accelerate:

Lighter particle ki λ zyada hoti hai (λ ∝ 1/√m)

5. Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle

Δx × Δp ≥ h / 4π

6. Photoelectric Effect

hν = φ + KE_max
KE_max = hν − hν₀ = h(ν − ν₀)
φ = hν₀ = hc / λ₀
Photoelectric — Slope = h, Threshold = ν₀, Y-intercept = −φ
Mains Trap — 2023 PYQ

λ₀ diya ho toh ν₀ = c / λ₀ — bacche direct λ₀ daal dete hain ν ki jagah.

Intensity ↑ → Electrons ki sankhya ↑, KE same

Frequency ↑ → KE ↑

Solved Numericals — 12 Types

🧮 Type 1: Bohr Radius (with Z)

Q: Find radius of 2nd orbit of Li²⁺ (Z=3).

  1. rₙ = 0.529 × n² / Z Å
  2. r₂ = 0.529 × 2² / 3
  3. r₂ = 0.529 × 4 / 3 = 0.705 Å
🧮 Type 2: Bohr Energy (with Z)

Q: Find energy of 2nd orbit of He⁺ (Z=2).

  1. Eₙ = −13.6 × Z² / n² eV
  2. E₂ = −13.6 × 4 / 4 = −13.6 eV
🧮 Type 3: Velocity of Electron

Q: Find velocity of electron in 2nd orbit of H atom.

  1. vₙ = 2.18 × 10⁶ × Z / n
  2. v₂ = 2.18 × 10⁶ × 1 / 2
  3. v₂ = 1.09 × 10⁶ m/s
🧮 Type 4: Frequency of Revolution (2026 PYQ)

Q: Find frequency of revolution for n=2, Z=1.

  1. f = 6.6 × 10¹⁵ × Z² / n³
  2. f = 6.6 × 10¹⁵ × 1 / 8
  3. f = 8.25 × 10¹⁴ Hz
🧮 Type 5: n+l Rule

Q: Which fills first: 4s or 3d?

  1. 4s: n+l = 4+0 = 4
  2. 3d: n+l = 3+2 = 5
  3. Lower n+l fills first → 4s first
🧮 Type 6: Cr Exception

Q: Write electronic configuration of Cr (24).

  1. Expected: [Ar] 3d⁴ 4s²
  2. Actual: [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹
  3. Reason: Half-filled d⁵ stable
🧮 Type 7: de Broglie (given v)

Q: Find λ of electron with v = 2.18 × 10⁶ m/s.

  1. λ = h / mv = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ / (9.1 × 10⁻³¹ × 2.18 × 10⁶)
  2. λ = 3.33 × 10⁻¹⁰ m = 3.33 Å
🧮 Type 8: de Broglie (accelerated by V — 2024 PYQ)

Q: Find λ of electron accelerated by 100 V.

  1. λ = 12.27 / √V Å
  2. λ = 12.27 / √100 = 12.27 / 10
  3. λ = 1.227 Å
🧮 Type 9: de Broglie Ratio

Q: Two particles (m and 4m) same V se accelerate. Find λ₁/λ₂.

  1. λ ∝ 1/√m (same V par)
  2. λ₁/λ₂ = √(m₂/m₁) = √(4m/m) = 2
  3. λ₁ : λ₂ = 2 : 1
🧮 Type 10: Photoelectric (λ₀ given — 2023 PYQ)

Q: Work function of metal = 2 eV. Find threshold wavelength.

  1. φ = hc / λ₀
  2. λ₀ = hc / φ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ × 3 × 10⁸) / (2 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)
  3. λ₀ = 6.21 × 10⁻⁷ m = 621 nm
🧮 Type 11: Nodes (2022 PYQ)

Q: Find total nodes in 3p orbital.

  1. n=3, l=1
  2. Radial = n−l−1 = 3−1−1 = 1
  3. Angular = l = 1
  4. Total = n−1 = 2
🧮 Type 12: Spectral Lines (2026 PYQ)

Q: How many spectral lines from n=4 to n=2?

  1. n₂=4, n₁=2
  2. (n₂−n₁)(n₂−n₁+1)/2 = (4−2)(4−2+1)/2 = 2×3/2
  3. = 3 lines
  4. Agar n(n−1)/2 lagaya toh 6 aa jata — galat.

KE = +, PE = double negative, Total = −

4. n+l Rule

Lower n+l pehle fill hota hai. Same n+l par lower n pehle.

4s (4) < 3d (5) < 4p (5) < 5s (5)

5. Nodes — 3d ka 0 Radial (2022 PYQ)

3d: n=3, l=2 → Radial = 3−2−1 = 0

Ground state (n₁=1): n(n−1)/2

Otherwise: (n₂−n₁)(n₂−n₁+1)/2

4th se 2nd → 3 lines (not 6)
