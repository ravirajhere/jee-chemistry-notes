Periodic Table & Periodicity

Modern Periodic Law, Atomic Radius, IE, EGA, EN, Exceptions, PYQ Analysis

1. Modern Periodic Law

Definition

Moseley ne 1913 mein bataya: "Properties of elements are periodic function of their Atomic Number."

Pehle Mendeleev ne Atomic Mass pe kiya tha, lekin usme anomalies thi (e.g., Ar vs K, Co vs Ni).

1.1 Important Points

  • Period = Horizontal row (7 periods)
  • Group = Vertical column (18 groups)
  • s-block → Group 1, 2
  • p-block → Group 13 to 18
  • d-block → Group 3 to 12
  • f-block → Lanthanides + Actinides

2. n + ℓ Rule (Aufbau Principle)

Rule

Electron pehle us orbital mein jaata hai jiska n + ℓ kam ho.

Agar n + ℓ same ho, toh n kam wala pehle fill hoga.

2.1 Important Orders

  • 4s < 3d (4s ka n+ℓ = 4, 3d ka n+ℓ = 5)
  • 6s < 4f (6s ka 4+0=4, 4f ka 4+3=7)
  • 4f < 5d (4f ka 4+3=7, 5d ka 5+2=7, par n kam hai 4f ka)
Mains Trap
  • Cr (24) → [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ (not 3d⁴ 4s²)
  • Cu (29) → [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ (not 3d⁹ 4s²)

Ye half-filled aur fully-filled stability ke karan hota hai.

3. Atomic Radius

Trends
  • Across Period (Left → Right): Radius decreases
  • Down Group (Top → Bottom): Radius increases

3.1 Types of Radius

TypeDefinition
Covalent RadiusHalf of distance between two bonded atoms
Van der Waals RadiusHalf of distance between two non-bonded atoms
Metallic RadiusHalf of distance between two metal atoms
Ionic RadiusCation < Neutral < Anion
Mains Trap
  • Cation chota (e⁻ lose)
  • Anion bada (e⁻ gain)
  • Isoelectronic species: Jitna zyada positive charge, utna chota radius

3.2 Lanthanide Contraction

  • 4f electrons poor shielding karte hain.
  • Isliye Zr ≈ Hf aur Mo ≈ W ka radius almost same hota hai.

4. Ionization Enthalpy (IE)

Definition

1 mole gaseous atom se 1 electron nikalne ki energy. Endothermic process hai.

4.1 Trends

  • Across Period: IE increases
  • Down Group: IE decreases

4.2 Exceptions (JEE Mains Favourite)

ElementExpectedActualReason
MgNa < MgNa < Al < Mg3s² full-filled
NC < N < OB < C < O < N2p³ half-filled
BeLi < BeLi < B < Be2s² full-filled
PSi < P < SSi < S < P3p³ half-filled
Mains Trap
  • IE₁ < IE₂ < IE₃ (Successive IE badhti hai)
  • Noble gases ki IE sabse zyada
  • Alkali metals ki IE sabse kam

5. Electron Gain Enthalpy (EGA)

Definition

1 mole gaseous atom mein 1 electron add karne par release hone wali energy. Exothermic process hai.

5.1 Trends

  • Across Period: EGA increases
  • Down Group: EGA decreases

5.2 Exceptions (JEE Mains Favourite)

  • Cl > F > Br > I (F ka exception)
  • Reason: F ka size chhota, inter-electronic repulsion zyada, EGA kam negative
Mains Trap
  • Noble gases ki EGA positive
  • Be, N, Ne → EGA almost zero ya positive

6. Electronegativity (EN)

Definition

Atom ki tendency electron ko apni taraf kheenchne ki. Pauling Scale sabse common hai.

6.1 Trends

  • Across Period: EN increases
  • Down Group: EN decreases

6.2 Important Orders

  • F > O > N > Cl
  • F > O > Cl > N > Br > I > S > C > P > H
  • Most EN: F (4.0)
  • Least EN: Cs (0.7)

7. Metallic & Non-Metallic Character

  • Across Period: Metallic ↓, Non-metallic ↑
  • Down Group: Metallic ↑, Non-metallic ↓
  • Most metallic: Cs
  • Most non-metallic: F
  • Metalloids: B, Si, Ge, As, Sb, Te

8. Isoelectronic Species

GroupSpecies

Mains Trap

Isoelectronic species mein radius compare karo: Jitna zyada positive charge, utna chota radius.

9. Diagonal Relationship

Pairs
  • Li – Mg
  • Be – Al
  • B – Si

Reason: Charge/Size ratio aur EN similar hoti hai.

10. IUPAC Nomenclature (Z > 100)

Rules
  • 0 → nil, 1 → un, 2 → bi, 3 → tri, 4 → quad
  • 5 → pent, 6 → hex, 7 → sept, 8 → oct, 9 → enn

Mains Trap

JEE Mains 2019 mein direct aaya tha: "IUPAC symbol for Z = 119?" → Uue

Mains Traps — Periodic Table

1. IE Exception

Na < Al < Mg — Mg ka 3s² full-filled hai

B < C < O < N — N ka 2p³ half-filled hai

2. EGA Exception

Cl > F > Br > I — F ka size chhota, inter-electronic repulsion zyada

3. Lanthanide Contraction

Zr ≈ Hf, Mo ≈ W — 4f electrons poor shielding karte hain

4. Isoelectronic Radius

Al³⁺ < Mg²⁺ < Na⁺ < F⁻ < O²⁻ < N³⁻

5. Diagonal Relationship

Li-Mg, Be-Al, B-Si — Similar properties

6. IUPAC Nomenclature

Z = 119 → Uue (Ununennium)

7. Cr & Cu Configuration

Cr → 3d⁵ 4s¹ (half-filled)

Cu → 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ (fully-filled)

8. Radius Order

Across Period: Na > Mg > Al > Si > P > S > Cl

Down Group: Li < Na < K < Rb < Cs

9. EN Order

F > O > N > Cl > Br > I

10. Metallic Character

Cs > Rb > K > Na > Li
