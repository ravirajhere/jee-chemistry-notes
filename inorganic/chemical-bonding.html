1. VSEPR Theory

1.1 Core Concept

VSEPR = Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion Theory.

Electron pairs (bonding + lone) maximum door rehna chahte hain. Shape isi se decide hota hai.

1.2 Notation: AXₘEₙ

SymbolMatlab
ACentral atom
XBonded atoms
ELone pairs on central atom
mNumber of X
nNumber of E

Example: H₂O = AX₂E₂

1.3 Repulsion Order

lp-lp > lp-bp > bp-bp

1.5 Edge Cases (99%ile Topic)

SF₄ — See-Saw Shape

SF₄ — See-Saw shape. Lone pair equatorial position mein, isliye axial bonds 90° se kam angle banate hain.

XeF₆ — Distorted Octahedral

XeF₆ — Distorted octahedral. Lone pair ki wajah se perfect octahedral nahi rehta. Steric number 7 → sp³d³.

2. Bent's Rule ⭐

Definition More electronegative substituent s-character kam wale hybrid orbital mein jaata hai.

Matlab: Agar central atom pe 2 substituents hain (ek electronegative, ek electropositive), toh electropositive wala zyada s-character lega.

2.1 Application 1: NH₃ vs NF₃ Dipole

MoleculeDipole (D)Reason
NH₃1.47N-H + lone pair same direction
NF₃0.23N-F + lone pair opposite direction

Explanation: N-H bond mein s-character kam hai. Lone pair zyada p-character lega. Isliye lone pair aur N-H bond same direction → dipole zyada.

2.2 Application 2: Bond Angle Order

H₂O (104.5°) > H₂S (92°) > H₂Se (91°)

2.3 Application 3: NH₃ vs PH₃

MoleculeBond AngleReason
NH₃107°sp³ (Bent's Rule)
PH₃93.5°Pure p-orbitals (Drago's Rule)
Cheat Code Bond angle ya dipole moment ka question aaye → Bent's Rule lagao. Central atom ke electronegativity ke hisaab se s-character decide karo.

3. Drago's Rule

Definition Jab central atom ka size bada ho aur electronegativity < 2.1 ho, toh hybridisation nahi hoti. Pure p-orbitals use hote hain, aur bond angle 90° ke paas hota hai.

3.1 Conditions

ConditionRequirement
Central atom sizeLarge
Electronegativity< 2.1
ConsequenceNo hybridisation, pure p-orbitals
Bond Angle~90°

3.3 Bent's Rule vs Drago's Rule

RuleKab LagaoExamples
Bent's RuleCentral atom small, EN highNH₃, H₂O, NF₃

Cheat Code NH₃ aur PH₃ compare karte waqt — NH₃ mein Bent's, PH₃ mein Drago's lagao.

4.4 Back Bonding (99%ile Topic)

Definition Electron density filled orbital se empty orbital mein jaati hai. Ye π-character deta hai aur molecule stable karta hai.

BF₃ — Back Bonding

BF₃ — F ka filled p-orbital se B ka empty p-orbital mein electron density donate hota hai. Isliye BF₃ Lewis acid hai.
Cheat Code Back bonding check karo jab central atom ke paas empty orbital ho aur ligand ke paas lone pair ho.

5. Molecular Orbital Theory

5.1 MOT Energy Order

For O₂, F₂, Ne₂:

σ1s < σ*1s < σ2s < σ*2s < σ2pz < π2px=π2py < π*2px=π*2py < σ*2pz

For N₂, C₂, B₂, Be₂, Li₂:

σ1s < σ*1s < σ2s < σ*2s < π2px=π2py < σ2pz < π*2px=π*2py < σ*2pz

5.2 Bond Order Formula

BO = ½ (Nb − Na) N_b = bonding electrons · N_a = antibonding electrons

5.3 N₂ MO Diagram

N₂ MO Diagram — Configuration: σ1s² σ*1s² σ2s² σ*2s² π2px² π2py² σ2pz² · BO = 3 · Diamagnetic

5.4 O₂ MO Diagram

O₂ MO Diagram — 2 unpaired electrons in π*2p. Configuration: σ1s² σ*1s² σ2s² σ*2s² σ2pz² π2px² π2py² π*2px¹ π*2py¹ · BO = 2 · Paramagnetic

6. Banana Bond (B₂H₆)

Definition 3-centre-2-electron (3c-2e) bond — 3 atoms, 2 electrons. Ye B₂H₆ (diborane) mein hota hai. Curved shape ki wajah se "banana bond" kehte hain.

6.1 Structure of B₂H₆

B₂H₆ (Diborane) — Bridge hydrogens 3c-2e bonds banate hain. Ye banana bonds hain.

6.2 Bonding Analysis

Bond TypeAtomsElectronsCount
2c-2eB-H (terminal)24 bonds
3c-2eB-H-B (bridge)22 bonds

Total electrons: 4 × 2 + 2 × 2 = 12 electrons

6.3 Other Examples

MoleculeBonding
B₂H₆3c-2e banana bonds
Al₂Cl₆3c-4e bridge bonds
BeCl₂ (dimer)3c-2e bridge bonds
Cheat Code B₂H₆ dekhte hi 2 banana bonds likho. Terminal 4, bridge 2.

7. Dipole Moment & Polarity

7.1 Formula

μ = q × d

Unit: Debye (D), 1 D = 3.336 × 10⁻³⁰ C·m

7.2 Polarity Rules

CaseμExample
Symmetrical0CO₂, CH₄, BF₃
Asymmetrical≠ 0H₂O, NH₃, CHCl₃

7.3 Dipole Moment Order

Ammonia Family

NH₃ (1.47) > NF₃ (0.23)

Reason: Bent's Rule — lone pair direction

Water Family

H₂O (1.85) > H₂S (0.95) > H₂Se (0.63)

Reason: Electronegativity difference

8. Fajan's Rule & Lattice Energy

Fajan's Rule Ionic bond mein covalent character kab badhta hai, ye batata hai.

8.1 4 Factors

FactorCovalent Character
Small cation↑
Large anion↑
High charge on cation↑
Pseudo noble gas config↑↑

Pseudo Noble Gas: Cu⁺, Ag⁺, Zn²⁺ (d¹⁰ config)

8.2 Polarizing Power Formula

Polarizing Power = Charge / (radius)² Chhota cation + high charge = zyada polarizing power = zyada covalent character

8.3 Lattice Energy Formula

U = (K × q₁ × q₂) / (r₁ + r₂)

8.4 Application: AlCl₃ vs MgCl₂ vs NaCl

CationChargeRadius (pm)Polarizing Power
Na⁺+11029.6 × 10⁻⁵
Mg²⁺+2723.86 × 10⁻⁴
Al³⁺+353.51.05 × 10⁻³

Order: AlCl₃ > MgCl₂ > NaCl (covalent character)

Cheat Code Lattice energy compare karte waqt, charge zyada important hai radius se. Charge double = lattice 4x.
